Medicine & Prescription Workflow

Medicines prescribed to outpatient patients will be sent to the pharmacist/dispensary.

Medicines prescribed to inpatient patients will be made available to the assigned duty nurse.

Prescription schedules will include: Morning, Afternoon, Evening and Night, along with the number of days.

The pharmacist will be able to update the medicine collection status later.
